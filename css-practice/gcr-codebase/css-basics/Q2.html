History of Agra

Agra is a historic city famous for its Mughal architecture, monuments and rich cultural heritage.

Famous Places

The Taj Mahal, Agra Fort and Mehtab Bagh are some of the popular attractions of Agra.

Culture

Agra is known for traditional food, handicrafts and historical architecture.
