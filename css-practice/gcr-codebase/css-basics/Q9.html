Technology Changes the Modern World

Technology has become an important part of everyday life. People use computers, smartphones and the internet for communication, education and entertainment.

Digital technology has also changed businesses. Companies use online platforms to communicate with customers and employees. This has created new opportunities for people around the world.

Education is another area that has benefited from technology. Students can access online courses, digital books and educational videos from almost anywhere.

Artificial intelligence is also becoming increasingly useful. It can help people analyse information, automate repetitive tasks and develop new solutions.

As technology continues to develop, people need to learn new skills. Digital literacy has become an important skill for students and professionals.
